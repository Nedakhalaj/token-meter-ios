# Token Meter

Token Meter is an iOS app that shows how much of your AI usage quota you've used, across Claude, Codex, OpenRouter and Google Drive, as simple progress bars on a dashboard and a home-screen widget.

You connect your own accounts. Each card shows your usage windows, when they reset, and when the numbers were last updated.

Everything runs on your iPhone. There is no Token Meter server and no Token Meter account.
