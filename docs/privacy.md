# Privacy Policy for Token Meter

Effective date: 25 October 2026

Token Meter is an iOS app made by Neda Khalajnejad. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## The short version

Token Meter does not collect, store, sell or share any of your data. There is no Token Meter server. Everything stays on your iPhone.

## What stays on your device

- **Sign-in credentials.** When you connect an account, the app stores the credential the provider gives it (an API key, a session cookie, or an access token) in the iOS Keychain on your device. Your passwords are never seen or stored by the app.
- **Usage numbers.** The app saves your accounts' names and latest usage numbers on your device, so they can be shown in the app and the home-screen widget.

Removing an account in the app deletes its credential and saved numbers from your device. Deleting the app removes everything.

## What is sent over the internet

The app only contacts the providers you connect, to read your own usage:

- OpenRouter (openrouter.ai)
- Anthropic Claude (claude.ai)
- OpenAI Codex (auth.openai.com, chatgpt.com)
- Google Drive (googleapis.com)

Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else. Each provider's own privacy policy applies to its service.

## Google user data

If you connect Google Drive, Token Meter requests the `drive.file` permission and uses it only to read your Drive storage quota (how much space is used and the total available), to show it as a bar in the app. The app does not read, create, change or delete any of your files. This data is kept only on your device and is never transferred to anyone.

Token Meter's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove Token Meter's access at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## Analytics and tracking

Token Meter has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Children

Token Meter is not directed at children under 13.
